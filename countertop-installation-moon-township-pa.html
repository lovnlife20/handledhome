Countertop FAQs

Do I need to supply the countertop material?

Yes — you pick the material and we handle the cutting, fitting, and installation. That way you get exactly the look you want.

Do you install stone or quartz countertops?

Full stone and quartz slabs are template-fabricated and installed by a slab fabricator due to weight and precision cutting. We install laminate, butcher block, and pre-fabricated solid-surface countertops, and can handle removal and plumbing reconnection around a fabricator's install.

Can you cut countertops to fit an unusual layout?

Yes — L-shaped runs, peninsulas, and odd angles are common and something we account for during the estimate.

Do you handle the plumbing hookup after the new countertop is in?

Yes, sink and faucet reconnection and leak-testing are part of the install.

How long does a countertop installation take?

A single-run kitchen or vanity countertop typically takes a few hours. Full kitchens with cutouts and seams take longer — we'll scope it during the estimate.
