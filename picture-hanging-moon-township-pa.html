Picture Hanging FAQs

Do I need to have the hardware already?

We bring standard picture-hanging hardware. For heavy pieces or masonry walls we will discuss what anchors are needed. Just have your frames and a general idea of where you want things.

Can you help me figure out the layout?

Yes. If you have a collection of frames and a general idea of where you want them, we can help work out the arrangement and spacing before putting anything in the wall.

How heavy is too heavy?

Most residential pieces we handle. Very large or exceptionally heavy installations — like large architectural mirrors or commercial signage — may require specific rigging. Let us know the size and weight and we can advise.

Can you combine this with TV mounting or other small jobs?

Yes. Picture hanging pairs naturally with TV mounting, shelf installation, and other small tasks in the same visit.

Picture Hanging and Art Installation for Moon Township Homes

Getting pictures and art hung correctly requires patience, a good eye, and knowledge of what's behind the wall you're drilling into. Moon Township homes run the gamut from new drywall over wood-frame construction to older plaster walls, and what works in one doesn't always work in the other. Plaster requires different anchors and a different drilling technique to avoid cracking, and heavy pieces in any wall type need to go into a stud or a proper anchor that can hold the weight over time — not just the day it goes up.

The most common picture-hanging calls we get in Moon Township are gallery walls — homeowners who have a collection of pieces to arrange and want them level, evenly spaced, and properly centered — and single large or heavy pieces like mirrors, oversized frames, and canvas art that the homeowner is nervous about hanging themselves. That anxiety is usually justified. A poorly chosen anchor in drywall will eventually fail, and a heavy mirror that drops from the wall isn't just a mess.

When we come out, we establish the right placement and height, locate studs or select the appropriate anchors for the wall type and the piece's weight, and hang everything level. For gallery walls, we lay out the arrangement on the floor first so spacing and composition can be confirmed before a single hole is made. We patch any holes that result from adjustments before we leave. You end up with everything on the wall, level, properly supported, and done right.
