What We Handle, and What Needs a Licensed Electrician

We install and replace battery-powered detectors, and can replace hardwired detectors on a like-for-like basis using existing wiring. If your home needs new hardwired detector circuits added where none exist, or other work requiring a licensed electrician, we will let you know plainly before starting.

Smoke detectors generally need replacement every ten years, and CO detectors have their own recommended lifespan — if you are not sure how old yours are, we can take a look and advise.

Smoke & CO Detector FAQs

How many smoke detectors does my house need?

It depends on the size and layout of your home. We can assess your home and recommend proper coverage.

Do you replace hardwired detectors?

Yes, we replace hardwired detectors on a like-for-like basis using the existing wiring.

Can you fix a detector that keeps chirping?

Yes, this is usually a battery, age, or wiring issue, and we can diagnose which it is.

Do you install carbon monoxide detectors too?

Yes, standalone and combination smoke/CO detectors are both something we install.

Smoke and CO Detector Installation in Moon Township

A lot of homes have detectors that are well past their recommended ten-year replacement age, even if they still technically work. Smoke detector sensors degrade over time, which means an old unit may not respond as quickly as it should. Replacing them is a small, inexpensive project with an outsized impact on home safety.

We also get a fair number of calls for the classic middle-of-the-night chirp — a detector that keeps beeping even after the battery has been replaced. That is usually a sign the unit itself has reached the end of its life rather than a battery problem, and swapping it out solves the issue for good.
