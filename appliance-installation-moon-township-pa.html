Installed and Checked Before We Leave

Appliance installation is straightforward when the connections are done correctly — the risk is a loose supply line or drain connection that causes a leak days later. We check every connection and run the appliance briefly before we consider the job finished.

This service covers standard hookups for existing supply lines and outlets. Appliances that need a new dedicated electrical circuit, new gas line, or other work requiring a licensed electrician or plumber are outside this scope, and we will let you know if that is the case before starting.

Appliance Installation FAQs

Do you install gas appliances?

For appliances connecting to an existing gas line, we can often help, but new gas line work requires a licensed plumber. We will let you know which applies to your situation.

Do you haul away my old appliance?

Yes, we can disconnect and remove the old appliance when we install the new one.

Can you install multiple appliances in one visit?

Yes, this is common, especially after a kitchen or laundry room update.

What if my appliance needs a new electrical circuit?

That work requires a licensed electrician. We will let you know if that applies before starting the job.

Appliance Installation in Moon Township

Most appliance installation calls come after a purchase from a big box store that delivers but does not hook the unit up, or after a move when washers, dryers, and dishwashers all need to be reconnected at once. We handle the connections and confirm everything runs correctly before we leave.
