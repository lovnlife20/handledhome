When to Replace Instead of Repair

  • Door is warped, cracked, or rotted through
  • Door is severely damaged from impact or water
  • Frame or jamb is damaged beyond adjustment
  • Door no longer fits the opening after settling
  • Exterior door is drafty and weatherstripping won't fix it
  • Door style is outdated and being replaced during a renovation
  • Pre-hung replacement is more cost-effective than repeated repairs
  • Selling or renting the home and the door needs to look right

Door Replacement FAQs

Do I need to buy the door myself?

Yes — we install doors that you supply. Pick it up from Home Depot, Lowe's, or any home center. We'll help you confirm the right size before you buy.

What's the difference between a slab door and a pre-hung door?

A slab door is just the door panel — it fits into an existing frame. A pre-hung door comes with the frame and hinges already attached and is used when the existing frame is damaged or removed.

Can you replace an exterior entry door?

Yes. We handle exterior door replacement including removal of the old door, installation, weatherstripping, and hardware. Note that structural or framing issues around the opening may require a licensed contractor.

Can I text photos for an estimate?

Yes — photos are the fastest way to get an estimate. Send a shot of the full door, the frame, and any damage. Text to 412-353-5341.

Do you also repair doors that don't need full replacement?

Yes. See our Doors & Trim repair page for sticking doors, hardware fixes, trim work, and other door repairs that don't require a full replacement.

How long does door replacement take?

Most single interior door replacements take two to three hours. Exterior doors typically take three to four hours depending on how the existing frame is set and whether the trim needs to be replaced. If we're replacing multiple doors in one visit, we can usually stack them efficiently. We'll give you a realistic time estimate before we schedule so you can plan your day.

Can you replace a door in an older Pittsburgh-area home where the opening may be out of square?

Yes — this comes up often in older homes throughout the area. When a rough opening is out of square, we shim and adjust the installation to get the door hanging and operating correctly. A slab door requires a more precise opening than a pre-hung door, so if the frame is significantly out of square we'll usually recommend going pre-hung. We'll assess the opening when we look at photos and give you a straight answer on what approach makes sense.

Door Replacement Cost in Moon Township

Several factors affect the cost of a door replacement. Here's what moves the number up or down.

Interior vs. Exterior

Interior doors are generally simpler and faster to install — hollow-core slabs, standard hardware, straightforward openings. Exterior doors involve weatherstripping, proper sealing, security hardware, and more exacting installation since they're exposed to weather. Exterior replacements take more time and involve more material.

Slab vs. Pre-Hung

A slab door drops into an existing frame and is the lower-cost option when the frame is in good shape. A pre-hung door comes with the jamb already attached and is needed when the frame is damaged, rotted, or the opening has shifted. Pre-hung takes more time to set and adjust than a slab swap.

Trim Replacement

If the existing door casing is damaged, rotted, or doesn't fit the new door cleanly, it needs to be replaced. Adding new trim to both sides of a door adds material and time. If your trim is in good shape and the new door matches the existing profile, we can often reuse it.

Hardware Choices

Basic hardware — passage knobs or lever handles — is straightforward. Keyed entry sets, deadbolts, smart locks, and multi-point locks each add time and cost. We install whatever hardware you supply. If you're not sure what you need, we can help you think through it before you buy.
