A Door That Keeps Water Where It Belongs

A shower door is only as good as its seal. Small gaps or worn seals let water escape onto the bathroom floor, which is both an annoyance and a slip hazard over time. We measure carefully and check the seal before finishing the install.

Frameless doors have become the more popular request in recent years for their cleaner look, but framed and sliding doors are still the right call in a lot of bathroom layouts. We can talk through which option makes sense for your space.

Shower Door FAQs

Do you install frameless shower doors?

Yes, along with framed and sliding shower doors.

Can you convert my shower curtain to a glass door?

Yes, this is one of our most common requests.

Do I need to buy the door first, or can you help me pick one?

We can install a door you have already purchased, or help point you toward the right style and size for your shower.

Why does my current shower door leak?

Usually a worn seal, a door that is not sitting level, or a track that has shifted — we can assess and repair or replace as needed.
