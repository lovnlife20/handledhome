About Mt. Lebanon

Mt. Lebanon is an Allegheny County municipality in the South Hills, developed as a streetcar suburb in the early 20th century and still defined by Washington Road as its main commercial corridor. The residential neighborhoods feature Tudor revivals, colonials, and arts-and-crafts bungalows built predominantly between the 1920s and 1940s for Pittsburgh professionals and their families. The architectural character is distinctive and well-preserved, which shapes the kind of repair work the homes require.

Original casement windows, plaster walls, wide trim profiles, and slate roofs are common in Mt. Lebanon housing. Owners here tend to have a repair-to-match instinct rather than a replace-with-whatever-is-cheapest approach, and that requires a contractor who understands the difference. Matching original trim profiles, patching plaster correctly, and repairing original woodwork rather than covering it with something generic is the standard we hold ourselves to on these jobs.
