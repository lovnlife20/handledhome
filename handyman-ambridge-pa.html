About Ambridge

Ambridge is a Beaver County borough built around the Ohio River and the legacy of the American Bridge Company, which located its headquarters here in the early 1900s. Merchant Street runs through the heart of the borough as its historic commercial corridor, and the surrounding residential streets are dense and walkable in a way that stands apart from the more spread-out communities nearby. The housing stock is predominantly 1910s through 1940s construction, built to house workers and their families in a compact, urban-adjacent layout.

Original plaster walls, hardwood trim, and millwork are still intact in many Ambridge homes that have stayed in the same family for generations. Where deferred maintenance has accumulated, it shows up in cracked plaster, failed caulking, sticking doors, and rotted wood at windows and entry points. Decks and additions from the 1970s and 1980s are past their useful life on many properties. The denser lot configuration also means access can be tighter than in suburban communities, and work has to be planned accordingly.

Handled Home serves Ambridge and the surrounding Beaver County area. We are about 25 minutes from Moon Township, and we are familiar with the repair profile common to older mill-era housing. We do careful work and understand how to make repairs that respect original materials rather than covering them over.
