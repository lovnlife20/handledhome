Services for Senior Homeowners

From safety modifications to general home maintenance, we help seniors stay comfortable and safe in their homes.

Grab Bar Installation

Install bathroom grab bars at tubs, showers, and toilets anchored into studs or with properly rated wall anchors to support body weight.

Stair & Ramp Safety

Repair loose stair railings, secure wobbly handrails, and assist with small ramp installations for step-free entry access.

Door Hardware Updates

Replace round knobs with lever handles that are easier to operate for seniors with limited hand strength or arthritis.

General Home Repairs

Fix dripping faucets, sticking doors, loose fixtures, broken hardware, and other small repairs that build up and become a safety concern.

Lighting Improvements

Install brighter bulbs, add under-cabinet lighting, or replace hard-to-reach fixtures so the home is properly lit and safer to navigate.

Home Maintenance Punch Lists

Work through a list of deferred maintenance items in one visit — the kind of things that pile up when keeping up with the house gets harder.

Senior Handyman FAQs

Are grab bars strong enough to support body weight?

When properly anchored into studs or with the right wall anchors, yes. Stud-mounted grab bars are the most secure. We assess the wall before installing to ensure each bar is mounted correctly.

Can you work through a punch list of small repairs?

Yes. Many senior homeowner visits involve working through a list of accumulated small repairs. That is a great use of a service call.

Can a family member be there or call in to discuss the work?

Absolutely. We are happy to communicate with adult children or family members coordinating on behalf of an elderly parent, either before or after the visit.

Are you insured?

Yes. Handled Home is fully insured and a registered PA contractor. We can provide proof of insurance if needed.
