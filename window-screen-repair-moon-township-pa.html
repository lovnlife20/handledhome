How Window Screen Repair Works

1. Send Photos or Describe the Job

Text us photos of the damaged screens or let us know how many need attention and what type of windows you have. For most screen jobs we can give you a clear ballpark without a site visit first. Call or text 412-353-5341.

2. We Measure and Assess On-Site

When we arrive we measure each opening and assess the frames. Frames that are still straight and solid get re-screened — new mesh and spline, frame reused. Frames that are bent beyond straightening or cracked get replaced. We carry standard aluminum frame stock and fiberglass screen mesh so most jobs can be completed in a single visit without waiting on a special order.

3. Done in One Visit

Most screen jobs — even several windows at once — are completed the same day. Each screen is tested to confirm it fits the window correctly, sits flat, and stays in the track before we leave. If you have other small repairs on the list, we can often knock those out in the same visit.

Window Screen FAQs

How much does window screen repair cost?

Re-screening a single window — removing the old mesh and spline, installing new fiberglass mesh and spline, trimming it flush — is a relatively affordable repair. Full frame replacement costs a bit more depending on the size and profile. The per-screen cost comes down when you batch several windows in one visit, which is the most efficient way to handle a house where multiple screens need attention. Text us the number of screens and a quick description and we can give you a ballpark before scheduling.

Do you carry screens with you or does it need to be ordered?

We carry standard aluminum frame stock and fiberglass screen mesh on the truck, so most re-screening and standard frame replacement jobs can be done in a single visit without waiting on materials. Non-standard sizes, specialty mesh types (solar screen, pet-resistant, etc.), or unusual frame profiles may require ordering ahead — we'll let you know after the initial assessment if that applies to your situation.

Can you re-screen just the mesh and keep the frame?

Yes, as long as the frame is still straight and in good condition. Re-screening is typically less expensive than a full replacement.

Do you do multiple windows in one visit?

Yes, and it's a good idea. Batching several screens together in one visit is efficient and keeps the per-screen cost lower.

What if I need an odd-size screen?

Custom sizes are not a problem. Screens can be cut and fitted to non-standard window openings.

Can you also fix the window itself?

We handle many common window repairs including stuck sashes, broken hardware, and trim issues. Let us know what else needs attention and we can often combine it in the same visit.

Window Screen Repair in Moon Township and Pittsburgh Area Homes

Window screens are one of those maintenance items that gets put off indefinitely. The screen is torn, the frame is bent, or it keeps popping out of the track — annoying but not urgent enough to jump the to-do list. Then spring arrives, you want to open the windows, and suddenly the screens are urgent. We handle screen repair and replacement for homeowners across Moon Township and the Pittsburgh area so you're not waiting until September to actually get it done.

In Moon Township, screen calls come from a few common situations: screens that were stored over winter and came out in spring more damaged than expected, older screens on homes in Carnot and along Flaugherty Run where the mesh has simply given out after years of use, and pet damage from dogs or cats that worked the mesh loose or tore through it. Screens that were cut out to install a window AC unit and never replaced are another frequent one — those tend to pile up over the years.

When we repair window screens, we assess whether the frame can be reused or needs to be replaced. In many cases only the mesh needs to go — the aluminum or vinyl frame is structurally fine, and the old spline just needs to be removed and new mesh and spline pressed in. Bent frames that can be straightened get straightened. Frames that are beyond saving get replaced. Every screen is tested to confirm it fits the window correctly and sits flat before we're done.
