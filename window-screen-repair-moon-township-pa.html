Window Screen Services

Most screen jobs are quick, affordable, and easy to batch with other small repairs around the house in the same visit.

Screen Re-Screening

Replace torn, hole-damaged, or worn-out screen mesh while keeping the existing frame if it is still in good shape.

Full Screen Replacement

New screen and frame when the original frame is bent, broken, or beyond saving. Cut and fitted to your window opening.

Screen Frame Repair

Straighten or repair bent aluminum frames so the screen fits properly and stays in the window track.

Pet-Resistant Screen

Heavier-duty screen mesh available for windows regularly used by pets that tend to push, scratch, or lean against screens.

Multiple Windows at Once

Replacing screens across multiple windows in one visit keeps cost down and gets the whole house done at once.

Screen Door Repair

Re-screen or replace screen doors, including slider tracks that stick or frames that have come off the door.

Common Screen Problems We Fix

  • Torn or punctured screen mesh
  • Holes from pets or accident damage
  • Bent or dented screen frames
  • Screens that won't stay in the track
  • Missing screens replaced
  • Screens that have pulled out of the spline
  • Faded or deteriorated old mesh
  • Sliding screen door issues
  • Screen door hardware problems
  • Screens loose in the window frame

Window Screen FAQs

Can you re-screen just the mesh and keep the frame?

Yes, as long as the frame is still straight and in good condition. Re-screening is typically less expensive than a full replacement.

Do you do multiple windows in one visit?

Yes, and it's a good idea. Batching several screens together in one visit is efficient and keeps the per-screen cost lower.

What if I need an odd-size screen?

Custom sizes are not a problem. Screens can be cut and fitted to non-standard window openings.

Can you also fix the window itself?

We handle many common window repairs including stuck sashes, broken hardware, and trim issues. Let us know what else needs attention and we can often combine it in the same visit.
