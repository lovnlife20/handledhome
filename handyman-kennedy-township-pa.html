About Kennedy Township

Kennedy Township is one of the smaller townships along the Ohio River in western Allegheny County, sandwiched between Coraopolis to the west and McKees Rocks to the east. It developed largely during the post-war industrial boom — homes built for the workers employed in the river valley industries that defined this corridor for generations. Those homes are well-built, but they're carrying 60 to 80 years of wear. Sticking doors from seasonal movement, settling cracks, aging decks, and fixtures that have outlived their useful life are all common calls from this part of the county.

Kennedy Township has a hillside character that many flat-state homeowners wouldn't expect — a lot of properties here sit on sloped lots with more exterior stairs, steeper driveways, and grade changes than you'd find in a newer suburban development. That affects what we see on service calls: exterior steps and railings that get heavy use and need maintenance, basement egress that involves more grade work, and decks that are elevated higher than in flat-lot communities and therefore show weather exposure more.

What We See Most in Kennedy Township Homes

Hillside Exterior Repairs

Many Kennedy Township homes sit on sloped lots, which means exterior stairs, retaining areas, and elevated decks are more common here than in flat developments. Stair railings that have rusted loose from their anchors, deck boards on elevated structures that have seen more weather than a grade-level patio, and handrails on hillside entry steps that get heavy use and need periodic re-securing are all frequent calls.

Doors That Have Never Fit Right

Mid-century homes on sloped lots often have doors that have never latched cleanly — the combination of original construction tolerances, decades of seasonal swelling, and subtle foundation movement means many interior and exterior doors in Kennedy Township homes require proper adjustment rather than just a new knob. We plane, plane, rehang, and re-strike doors correctly rather than just shaving a bit off the top and hoping for the best.

Deferred Maintenance Catch-Up

Kennedy Township has a significant population of long-term residents who have lived in their homes for decades. The repair calls we get here are often comprehensive — a full day of work covering dripping faucets, light fixtures that have burned out and never been replaced, trim that's been loose for years, and flooring that's wanted replacement for a decade. One visit can make a significant difference in a home that has been running behind on maintenance.

Newer Buyers Updating Older Homes

Kennedy Township's proximity to Pittsburgh proper and its comparatively affordable housing has brought younger buyers to the area — people buying mid-century homes and updating them to current standards. We see a lot of these jobs: flooring replacement throughout, bathroom fixture upgrades, TV mounting and smart home installation, and the general cosmetic work that brings an older home in line with how the new owners want to live.
