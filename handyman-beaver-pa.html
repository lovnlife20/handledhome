About Beaver

Beaver Borough sits at the confluence of the Beaver River and the Ohio, directly across the water from Monaca and about 25 miles northwest of Pittsburgh. It's the Beaver County seat, and it carries itself like one — Third Street is a genuine historic commercial corridor, the courthouse anchors the downtown, and the residential streets that climb the hillside above the river are lined with Victorian and early 20th century homes that are among the most architecturally distinct in the county. This is not a bedroom suburb that grew up around an interstate exit. Beaver has its own identity, its own downtown, and a housing stock that reflects more than a century of careful ownership.

That housing age means a specific set of priorities when something needs repaired. Victorian-era homes and the Craftsman and colonial revival houses built through the 1910s and 1930s have original woodwork, wide trim profiles, and architectural details that took real skill to build. Owners of these homes want someone who understands what they have and can work with it — not a quick patch and a generic replacement. Exterior trim repair and door work on a 100-year-old home is a different job than the same work on a 1990s colonial, and Pittsburgh's freeze-thaw cycle is particularly hard on older painted wood. Deferred maintenance on exterior trim compounds fast once moisture gets a foothold behind the paint line.

Brighton Township surrounds the borough on three sides and tells a completely different story. The township's residential development runs from postwar ranches and split-levels through the subdivisions that filled in from the 1970s onward — newer construction, standard materials, builder finishes that are now 30 to 50 years old and due for attention. Handled Home is based in Moon Township, about 25 minutes southeast. We serve both sides of that divide regularly — careful trim and carpentry work in the older borough homes, and the fixture upgrades, flooring installs, and deck repairs that Brighton Township homeowners are working through.

Do you work on Victorian-era or historic homes?

Yes — and it's some of our most common work in Beaver Borough. Older homes with original woodwork, wide trim profiles, plaster walls, and period doors require a different approach than standard repairs on newer construction. We don't treat original trim as something to rip out and replace with MDF. If the material is worth saving, we repair it. If it needs replacing, we match the profile as closely as the job calls for. If you have a home on the hill above Third Street and you've been hesitant to call someone because you're worried they won't understand what they're looking at — that's exactly who we're here for.
