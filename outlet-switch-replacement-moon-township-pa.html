What We Handle, and What Needs a Licensed Electrician

This service covers replacing an existing outlet or switch in its current location using the wiring already there — a straightforward, common repair. If a job turns out to need a new circuit, panel work, or anything beyond a like-for-like swap, that requires a licensed electrician, and we will tell you plainly if that is the case rather than doing work outside what is appropriate.

Warm or discolored outlets can be a sign of a real electrical problem, not just cosmetic wear — if that is what you are seeing, mention it when you reach out so we can prioritize looking at it.

Outlet & Switch FAQs

Is a simple outlet swap something you can do, or does it need an electrician?

A like-for-like outlet or switch replacement using existing wiring is something we handle. New circuits or panel work require a licensed electrician.

Can you install GFCI outlets?

Yes, GFCI outlet installation in kitchens, bathrooms, and other required areas is a common request.

Do you install USB outlets?

Yes, upgrading a standard outlet to include USB charging ports is straightforward in most cases.

My outlet feels warm — is that a problem?

That can indicate a real electrical issue. Let us know when you reach out so we can take a closer look and advise honestly on next steps.
