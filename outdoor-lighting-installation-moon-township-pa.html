Outdoor Lighting FAQs

Do I need to supply the light fixtures?

Yes — you pick the fixtures and we handle the mounting and wiring, so you get exactly the look you want.

Can you run new electrical circuits for outdoor lighting?

We work with existing wiring and circuits — swapping fixtures, adding low-voltage landscape lighting, and connecting to existing outdoor outlets. New circuit runs from the panel require a licensed electrician.

Do you install low-voltage landscape lighting kits?

Yes, including customer-supplied kits — laid out, staked, and connected to the transformer.

Can you hang string lights on a pergola or deck?

Yes, that's one of our most common outdoor lighting requests, especially paired with our Pergola Installation and Deck Repair services.

How long does outdoor lighting installation take?

A single fixture swap is usually under an hour. Patio string lights or landscape lighting runs take longer depending on the layout — we'll scope it during the estimate.
