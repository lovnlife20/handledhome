Small Repairs That Solve an Annoying Problem

Storm and screen doors get used constantly and take the brunt of wind and weather, so hardware wears out faster than on a standard door. A door that slams shut hard enough to shake the frame usually just needs a new closer — a quick, inexpensive fix.

This is a different service from our door replacement work, which covers installing a brand-new storm or entry door. This page is focused on repairing the door you already have.

How the Process Works

1. Send Photos

2. Diagnose the Issue

3. Repair & Adjust

Storm and Screen Door Repair in Moon Township

The most common storm door call we get is a slamming door — the closer has worn out or lost its hydraulic resistance, so the door swings shut hard instead of easing closed. It is a small part, but it makes a noticeable difference in how the door feels to use every day.

Screens are the other frequent repair, especially after a summer of regular use or a run-in with a pet. Whether the screen has torn, the frame has bent, or the rollers have worn out, we can usually repair the existing door rather than replacing the whole unit.
