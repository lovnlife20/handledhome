About Oakdale

Oakdale is a small borough tucked along Chartiers Creek in western Allegheny County, sitting on Route 50 between Carnegie to the east and McDonald to the west. It's a tight community — a compact grid of streets, a modest downtown, and residential neighborhoods that grew up in the decades after World War II when working families settled this stretch of the valley. The homes here reflect that era and that character: well-built, owner-occupied, and aging in the way that modest postwar and mid-century construction does when it's been in the same family for 40 or 50 years.

That housing profile produces a familiar and specific repair list. Homes from the 1950s through the 1970s still have original windows and trim in many cases — wood that has been painted over many times and is now starting to fail at the edges. Decks added in the 1980s and 1990s are now 30 to 40 years old and past their practical life in the boards and hardware if not in the framing. Doors that settled decades ago and were never quite adjusted. Fixtures that have never been replaced. Deferred maintenance is a natural consequence of long-term ownership — the house was always fine enough that the repairs kept getting pushed back. Until now.

Oakdale is minutes from our Moon Township base — it's on the same Route 50 corridor as North Fayette, and we're in the area constantly. There's no travel premium, no scheduling delay from distance, and no learning curve on the housing stock. We work on Chartiers Creek valley homes regularly and know exactly what they tend to need.
