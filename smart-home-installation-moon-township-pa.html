Note on Electrical Work

Smart home device installation typically involves low-voltage wiring — doorbell wires, thermostat wires, and data cables — which falls within standard handyman scope.

New circuit installation, panel work, or anything requiring a licensed electrician is outside our scope and we will let you know upfront if your project requires it.

Smart Home Installation FAQs

Do I need to buy the devices first?

Yes, you purchase the devices and we install them. If you are unsure which model to get, let us know what you are trying to do and we can help point you in the right direction.

Do you set up the app too?

Basic app setup and device pairing is included. Full network configuration or troubleshooting complex home automation setups may be outside scope for a standard visit.

My old thermostat doesn't have a C wire. Is that a problem?

Many smart thermostats include a C-wire adapter that solves this. We check your existing wiring first and work with what's there.

Can you install multiple devices in one visit?

Yes. Installing a thermostat, doorbell, and a couple of cameras in a single visit is a common and efficient way to do it.
