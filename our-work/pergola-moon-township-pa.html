Our Work — Pergola Project

Pergola Installation Project in Moon Township, PA

A Moon Township homeowner had purchased a pergola kit and needed it assembled and installed correctly on their patio — level, square, and solid enough to stand up to Western PA weather. They wanted it done right the first time, without the project dragging across multiple weekends. We handled the full installation start to finish.

What We Did

We assembled the homeowner's pergola kit and installed it over their existing patio. That meant laying out and squaring the footprint, setting and anchoring the posts plumb, building up the frame, and fitting the hardtop roof and mosquito netting according to the kit's design. We checked every connection for level and stability as we went, and cleaned up the work area completely before we left. The homeowner had a finished, ready-to-use pergola by the end of the job — no loose ends, no second trip.
