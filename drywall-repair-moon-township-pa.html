Drywall Repair Services

Drywall damage is one of those things you see every day once you notice it. We repair the damage, smooth the area, and get it ready for paint.

Hole Patching

Repair doorknob holes, accidental damage, cutouts, dents, and other drywall holes.

Crack Repair

Fix drywall cracks around walls, ceilings, corners, seams, and high-stress areas.

Ceiling Repair

Repair ceiling holes, cracks, damaged panels, and problem areas from previous repairs.

Water-Damage Patches

Replace damaged drywall sections after leaks have been repaired and the area is dry.

Paint-Ready Finishing

Mud, tape, sand, and finish the area so it is ready for primer and paint.

Small Room Repairs

Handle multiple patches, wall damage, or punch-list drywall work in one visit when possible.

Common Drywall Problems We Fix

  • Doorknob holes
  • Small and medium wall holes
  • Drywall cracks
  • Ceiling cracks
  • Water-damaged drywall
  • Damaged drywall corners
  • Bad previous patch repairs
  • Drywall seams showing
  • Nail pops and screw pops
  • Texture issues where possible
  • Cutouts from plumbing or electrical work
  • Move-in and move-out wall repairs

Paint-Ready Does Not Always Mean Painted

Most drywall repairs include patching, taping, mudding, sanding, and leaving the repaired area ready for primer and paint.

Painting can sometimes be included depending on the job, but it should be discussed up front. Matching existing wall paint is not always perfect, especially if the paint has faded, aged, or was not recently applied.

How the Drywall Repair Process Works

1. Send Photos

Clear photos help us understand the size, location, and type of repair needed.

2. Review the Repair

We look at the damage and explain what likely needs to be patched, replaced, or finished.

3. Patch & Finish

We complete the repair, apply mud and tape where needed, and sand the area for a cleaner finish.

Drywall Repair FAQs

Can you repair small drywall holes?

Yes. We handle small holes, doorknob damage, dents, cracks, and other common drywall repairs.

Do you repair ceilings?

Yes. We repair ceiling cracks, holes, damaged drywall sections, and some water-damage areas once the leak has been fixed.

Is painting included?

Not automatically. We can discuss painting depending on the repair, but most drywall work is quoted as paint-ready unless painting is specifically included.

Can I text photos for an estimate?

Yes. Photos help a lot. Send close-up photos and a wider shot of the wall or ceiling so we can understand the repair better.
