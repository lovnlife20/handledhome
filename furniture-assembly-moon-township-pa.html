Common Furniture Assembly Situations

  • Confusing or missing assembly instructions
  • Missing or incorrect hardware in the box
  • Furniture that wobbles once assembled
  • Large or heavy items like wardrobes and bed frames
  • Not having the right tools on hand
  • Furniture that needs to be disassembled for a move
  • Tall furniture that is not anchored for tip-over safety
  • Multiple pieces that need to be assembled at once

Assembled Right the First Time

Flat-pack furniture is designed to be assembled once, correctly. Loose screws, missing anchoring, or a rushed assembly job can leave furniture wobbly or, in the case of tall dressers and bookshelves, unsafe.

We bring our own tools, follow the manufacturer instructions, and anchor tall furniture to the wall where it is designed for anti-tip hardware — an easy step to skip that matters a lot in homes with kids.
