Siding Protects What Is Behind It

Siding is your home's first line of defense against water, and a small crack or gap does not stay small for long. Once water gets behind siding, it can damage the sheathing and framing underneath, so repairs are worth handling before winter or before the next storm.

For most repairs, we replace only the damaged panels rather than the whole wall. We do our best to match the existing color and profile, though some fading is expected on older siding.

How the Process Works

1. Send Photos

Photos of the damaged area and, if known, the siding brand or color help us plan the repair.

2. Assess the Damage

We check whether the issue is cosmetic or if water has already reached the trim or sheathing behind it.

3. Repair & Reseal

We replace or re-secure the affected siding and reseal seams so water stays out.

Siding Repair in Moon Township Homes

Most of our siding calls start with something small — a cracked panel, a corner that has pulled loose, a section that rattles every time the wind picks up. Left alone, these small issues let water get behind the siding, which can lead to rot in the trim and sheathing underneath. Catching it early keeps the repair simple.

Storm damage is the other common reason we get called out, especially after high wind events come through the Pittsburgh area. A single loose or missing panel is a quick fix, but it is worth handling before the next storm makes the damage worse.
