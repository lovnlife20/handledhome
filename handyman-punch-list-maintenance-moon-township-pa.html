Punch List & Maintenance Visit FAQs

Can you handle a long list of small repairs in one visit?

Yes — that's the point of this service. Send us the full list and we'll scope out how much we can realistically knock out in a single appointment.

Do you work with property managers on multiple properties?

Yes, we work with landlords and property managers on both one-time punch lists and ongoing maintenance across multiple units or properties.

Can you do a repair list before I sell my house?

Yes, pre-sale repair lists from a home inspection or agent walkthrough are a common request. See our Home Repairs Before Selling guide for what typically comes up.

Do you handle move-in and move-out punch lists for rentals?

Yes, including fast turnaround between tenants — see our Rental Property Turnover Repairs service.

Is this different from calling about a single repair?

Yes. This service is built around packaging several items into one visit rather than a single-issue call, which is usually more efficient for both of us when you have more than one thing that needs attention.

Punch List & Maintenance Visits for Moon Township Homeowners and Property Managers

Most households and rental properties end up with a running list of small things that need attention — a loose cabinet hinge here, a dripping faucet there, a screen that needs replacing, a step that feels a little soft. None of it is urgent on its own, which is exactly why it tends to sit for months. Packaging that list into one visit is usually far more efficient than scheduling a separate call for each item.

The same package framing works well for move-in and move-out situations, pre-sale repair lists from a home inspection, and rental turnover between tenants — all cases where a homeowner or property manager has a defined list of items and a deadline to get them done. For property managers handling multiple units, we can also set up a recurring visit schedule rather than reacting to one-off calls.

If you have a list that needs handling, reach out to Handled Home. We're based locally in Moon Township and work throughout the Pittsburgh area. Call or text 412-353-5341, or fill out the estimate form below.
