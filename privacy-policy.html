Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Information

Handled Home is a small, locally owned handyman business in Moon Township, Pennsylvania. We collect the minimum we need to give you an estimate and do the work. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone who isn't helping us run the business.

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Information We Collect

Information you give us

When you submit an estimate request or contact form on this site, we collect your name and phone number (both required), and your email address, job address, and project description if you choose to provide them. If you call or text us at 412-353-5341, we have your phone number and whatever you send us, including any project photos.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, handledhome.net uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site. This collects your IP address, approximate location (city level), device and browser type, the pages you view, and how you arrived. We also record a small number of specific actions: clicking a phone number, clicking a text-message link, opening our Our Work page, and submitting a contact form. These are recorded as anonymous events — they are not tied to your name.

What we don't collect

We do not collect payment information through this website. We do not ask for Social Security numbers, dates of birth, or any government identification. There are no accounts to create and no passwords to store.

How We Use It

To respond to you and do the work

We use your contact details to reply to your request, ask follow-up questions, provide an estimate, send a written contract, schedule the job, and follow up afterward — including about warranty work covered by our one-year guarantee.

To improve the site

Analytics data tells us which services people are looking for and which pages are useful, so we can write better ones. We look at it in aggregate, not to track individual people.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not add you to a marketing list without you asking. We do not send mass promotional texts or emails.

Services We Rely On

Running the site means a few outside companies necessarily handle some data. Here is the complete list.

Web3Forms — contact form delivery

Our contact and estimate forms are processed by Web3Forms, which passes the submission along to our email inbox. Your form contents pass through their service in order to reach us.

Google Analytics — site statistics

Google Analytics 4 collects the usage information described above and sets cookies in your browser to recognize repeat visits. Google's own privacy policy governs what they do with it.

Google Fonts — site typeface

The site loads its typeface from Google's font servers, which means your browser makes a request to Google and your IP address is visible to them as part of that request.

GitHub Pages and Cloudflare — hosting and security

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which sits in front of it to speed up loading and block malicious traffic. Both keep standard server logs that include visitor IP addresses.

Email and phone

Our email and phone service providers necessarily process the messages you send us, the same way they would for any business.

Cookies and Your Choices

Cookies

The only cookies this site sets are Google Analytics cookies used to measure visits. There is no advertising network on this site and no cross-site ad tracking. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, and the site will still work normally. Google also publishes a browser add-on that opts you out of Analytics entirely.

Do Not Track

Browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal inconsistently and there is no agreed standard for honoring it, so this site does not respond to it. Blocking cookies is the more reliable option.

Text messages

If you text us, we'll text you back about your project. That's the extent of it — we don't run marketing text campaigns. Reply STOP at any time and we'll stop texting you.

Seeing or deleting your information

Email info@handledhome.net or call 412-353-5341 and ask what we have on file, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. We'll take care of it. We may need to keep records tied to a completed job — contracts, invoices, and warranty history — for as long as the law and our insurance require.

Other Things Worth Saying

How long we keep information

Estimate requests that don't turn into work are kept only as long as they're useful, and are discarded when they aren't. Records for completed jobs are retained for business, tax, warranty, and insurance purposes.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and form submissions are encrypted in transit. No method of transmission over the internet is perfectly secure, so please don't send us sensitive personal information — we don't need it to fix your deck.

Children

This site is intended for homeowners and property managers. It is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to outside resources such as manufacturer instructions or municipal permit information. Once you follow a link off handledhome.net, that site's privacy practices apply, not ours.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. There's no archive of prior versions — the page you're reading is the current policy.

Questions About Any of This?

Email info@handledhome.net or call/text 412-353-5341. You can also send a message here.
