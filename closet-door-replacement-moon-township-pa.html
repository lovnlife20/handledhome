Small Doors, Daily Frustration

Closet doors get opened and closed constantly, so worn hardware and misaligned tracks show up fast. A bifold that jumps its track or a sliding door that sticks is one of those small annoyances people put up with for years.

In many cases the fix is simpler than a full replacement — a new track, roller, or guide can solve the problem. When the doors themselves are warped, damaged, or outdated, we handle the full replacement and make sure the new doors are properly aligned and trimmed to fit.

Closet Door FAQs

Do you replace both bifold and sliding closet doors?

Yes, we replace and repair bifold, sliding, hinged, and mirror closet doors.

Can you match my existing hardware or door style?

We do our best to match existing hardware and finishes, or help you pick a comparable style if the original is discontinued.

Do you install mirror closet doors?

Yes, including proper alignment and secure mounting so they hang safely.

Can you just fix the track instead of replacing the whole door?

Often, yes. If the doors themselves are in good shape, a track or hardware repair may be all that is needed.
