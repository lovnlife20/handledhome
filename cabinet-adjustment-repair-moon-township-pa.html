Cabinets Take a Lot of Daily Wear

Kitchen and bathroom cabinets get opened, closed, and loaded up every single day, so hinges wear out and drawer slides eventually fail. Most of the time these are adjustment or hardware repairs, not a sign the cabinets need to be replaced.

We diagnose whether a door needs a hinge adjustment, a new hinge entirely, or if the cabinet box itself has shifted. For drawers, we check the slides, glides, and mounting before recommending a fix.

Cabinet Repair FAQs

Can you fix cabinet doors that do not line up?

Yes. Most misaligned cabinet doors can be adjusted at the hinge without any other work needed.

Do you replace cabinet hardware?

Yes, we replace knobs, pulls, handles, and hinges, including upgrading to soft-close hardware.

Can you fix drawers that will not slide properly?

Yes. We repair or replace drawer slides and glides so drawers open and close smoothly again.

Do you install brand-new cabinets?

This service is focused on adjustments and repairs to existing cabinets. For full cabinet installs, give us a call and we can talk through your project.

Cabinet Repairs in Moon Township Kitchens and Baths

Cabinet hinges are a wear item, and most homes will need a hinge adjustment or replacement at some point. What starts as a door that swings slightly crooked usually gets worse — catching on the frame, no longer closing flush, or eventually the hinge failing outright. Catching it early with a simple adjustment is a lot less work than dealing with a door that has come loose entirely.

We see a lot of drawer slide issues too, especially in kitchens that get heavy daily use. Old metal slides wear out, plastic guides crack, and drawers start sticking or dropping at an angle when pulled out. In most cases we can replace just the slide hardware rather than the whole drawer box.
