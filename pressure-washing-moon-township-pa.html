The Right Pressure for the Surface

Pressure washing makes a bigger difference than most homeowners expect — years of mold, algae, and grime buildup can make a house look dated even when nothing is actually wrong with it. The tricky part is using the right pressure and technique for each surface, since too much pressure can damage siding, wood decking, or mortar joints.

We adjust pressure and cleaning solution based on what we are washing, whether that is delicate vinyl siding, a wood deck, or a concrete driveway that can handle more force.

Pressure Washing FAQs

Will pressure washing damage my siding or deck?

Not when done correctly. We adjust pressure and technique to the surface being cleaned.

Can you wash before I paint or stain?

Yes, pre-paint washing is a common request and helps the new coat adhere better.

Do you use chemicals or just water pressure?

For mold and algae, a cleaning solution combined with pressure washing gets better, longer-lasting results than water alone.

How often should I get my house pressure washed?

Most homes benefit from washing every one to two years, though shaded or damp areas may need it more often.
