About Sewickley

Sewickley Borough sits along the Ohio River about 12 miles northwest of Pittsburgh, connected to the city by Route 65. Broad Street is a genuine main street — walkable, independent, the kind of commercial corridor that draws people who could live anywhere and choose to be here. The residential streets off Broad Street are lined with Victorian and colonial revival homes from the late 1800s through the 1930s, built during the era when Pittsburgh's business class settled the Ohio River communities north of the city. These are not starter homes or quick builds. They were constructed with care and have been maintained with care, which means when something finally needs repaired, the owner wants it done the same way.

That housing profile produces a specific set of priorities. Original woodwork and wide trim profiles that have survived a century and deserve to survive another one. Plaster walls. Doors and windows that have settled with the house over decades. Exterior wood that Pittsburgh's freeze-thaw cycle works on relentlessly. When trim is rotted or a door no longer closes right on a home like this, the answer isn't a generic replacement — it's a repair that respects what's there, or a replacement that matches it. We approach older Sewickley homes with that instinct: repair over replace where the material warrants it, match profiles when it doesn't, and don't treat a hundred-year-old house like new construction.

Edgeworth and Sewickley Heights sit directly adjacent to the borough and represent a different scale — larger lots, more substantial homes, and exterior projects that run bigger. Deck repair and building, exterior trim work, and comprehensive punch-list visits are common calls from that area. Handled Home comes out of Moon Township along Route 65, about 20 minutes away. We serve the full Sewickley area including Edgeworth, Sewickley Heights, and Sewickley Hills, with no travel premium and no minimum job size.
