What We Handle, and What Needs a Licensed Plumber

This service covers replacing an existing garbage disposal using the sink's existing drain and electrical connection — a straightforward swap that does not require new plumbing or wiring. If your situation involves running a new dedicated circuit, altering drain lines, or other work that requires a licensed electrician or plumber, we will let you know before starting and can point you toward the right person.

Most disposal replacements are a same-visit job. We remove the old unit, install the new one, and test it under the sink before we consider the job done.

How the Process Works

1. Send Photos

Photos of your current disposal and under-sink setup help us confirm the scope.

2. Confirm Compatibility

We check that the new disposal fits your existing drain and electrical connection.

3. Install & Test

We install the new disposal, reconnect everything, and run water to check for leaks.

Garbage Disposal FAQs

Do I need to buy the disposal, or can you supply it?

We can install a disposal you provide, or help you pick the right model for your needs.

Can you fix my disposal instead of replacing it?

If it is a jam or a simple issue, we will check first — replacement is usually only needed when the motor has failed.

Is this something a plumber has to do?

A standard disposal swap using existing connections does not require a licensed plumber. If your situation needs new plumbing work, we will let you know.

How long does a disposal replacement take?

Most replacements are completed in under an hour once we are on site.

Garbage Disposal Replacement in Moon Township

Garbage disposals typically last eight to fifteen years, and the failure mode is usually pretty clear — the unit hums without spinning (a jammed or burned-out motor), it leaks from the bottom (a failed seal), or it simply stops responding. In most cases, replacement is faster and more reliable than continuing to repair an aging unit.

We handle the straightforward swap: disconnecting the old disposal, installing the new one, and reconnecting the drain and electrical using your existing setup. If a job turns out to need new wiring or plumbing, we will tell you plainly rather than working outside what is appropriate for a handyman to take on.
