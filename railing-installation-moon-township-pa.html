Railing FAQs

Do I need to rebuild my whole porch or deck to get new railing?

No — replacing just the railing is one of the most common requests we get, and it doesn't require rebuilding the structure underneath unless we find something else going on once we're into it.

Do you install both vinyl and aluminum railing?

Yes, along with wood railing repair. We can talk through the tradeoffs of each material for your porch or deck during the estimate.

Can you add a handrail to stairs that don't have one?

Yes, adding a stair handrail where none exists is a common and worthwhile safety upgrade.

Do you follow code guidelines for railing height and spacing?

Yes, we install with standard safety guidelines for height and baluster spacing in mind. Larger structural changes may require a permit, which we'll flag before starting.

Can you fix just one loose or wobbly section?

Yes, most railing repairs are localized to the section that's actually loose rather than the entire railing run.

Railing Installation and Repair for Moon Township Homeowners

Railing is one of those things people often don't think to replace on its own — they assume new railing means a full porch or deck rebuild. In most cases it doesn't. Whether the existing railing has come loose, no longer meets a safety standard, or you just want a material upgrade to vinyl or aluminum, we can handle the railing on its own.

Safety is usually the real driver behind a railing call, especially when a section has become loose or a set of stairs never had a handrail to begin with. That safety angle matters even more for households planning to age in place — see our Handyman Services for Seniors page for other safety-focused upgrades we handle.
