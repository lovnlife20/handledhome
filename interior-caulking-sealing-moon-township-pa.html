A Small Detail That Changes How a Room Looks

Caulk lines are easy to ignore until they crack, discolor, or grow mold — and then they are hard to miss. A clean, straight caulk line around a tub or backsplash makes the whole room look more finished, while cracked or moldy caulk makes even a clean bathroom look neglected.

This is a different service from our exterior caulking and weatherproofing work, which focuses on siding, trim, and windows on the outside of the home. This page covers interior caulking — bathrooms, kitchens, trim, and countertops.

How the Process Works

1. Send Photos

Photos of the area — tub, backsplash, trim, or window — help us scope the caulking job.

2. Prep the Area

We remove old or damaged caulk and clean the surface so the new caulk adheres properly.

3. Apply & Finish

We apply a clean, even bead of caulk and tool it for a smooth, finished line.

Interior Caulking FAQs

Do you remove old caulk before re-caulking?

Yes. Removing old, cracked, or moldy caulk first gives the new caulk a clean surface to adhere to.

Can you fix moldy bathroom caulk?

Yes, we remove moldy caulk and re-seal the area to help prevent it from coming back as quickly.

Do you caulk baseboards after flooring installs?

Yes, caulking baseboard and trim is a common follow-up to a new floor or trim installation.

Is this different from your exterior caulking service?

Yes. This page covers interior caulking — bathrooms, kitchens, and trim. Our exterior caulking and weatherproofing service covers siding, exterior trim, and windows outside.

Interior Caulking and Sealing in Moon Township Homes

Bathroom caulk is the most common reason we get called for interior sealing work. Tub and shower caulk takes on constant moisture, and even good-quality caulk eventually cracks, discolors, or grows mold along the seam. Removing the old caulk and starting fresh usually solves the problem for good, rather than caulking directly over what is already there.

The other common request is finish work after a renovation — a new backsplash that was never caulked to the counter, baseboard that went in without a caulked seam, or window trim with a visible gap. These are quick fixes that make a big difference in how finished a room looks.
