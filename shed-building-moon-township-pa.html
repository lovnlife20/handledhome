Why Proper Shed Installation Matters

A shed that is not properly leveled, anchored, or set on a stable base can shift, lean, and develop door and framing problems over time.

Getting the base and anchoring right from the start prevents those issues and keeps your shed looking and working right for years. We make sure the site is prepared correctly before anything goes up.

Shed Building FAQs

Do you build sheds from scratch?

Yes. We can build a custom shed to your specifications, including size, door placement, windows, roof style, and interior layout.

Can you assemble a prefab shed kit?

Yes. We assemble most kit and box sheds, including brands from big-box stores. We handle the site prep, assembly, leveling, and anchoring.

Do I need a permit for a shed in Moon Township?

Permit requirements depend on shed size and placement. Some accessory structures require permits in Pennsylvania. We recommend checking with Moon Township directly before the project starts.

Can I text photos for an estimate?

Yes. Photos of the area where the shed will go are very helpful. Send us clear pictures of the site and we can give you a much more accurate estimate before we even come out.
