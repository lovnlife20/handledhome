About Baden

Baden is a small river borough in Beaver County, situated along the Ohio River between Ambridge and Conway. Like many communities in this part of western Pennsylvania, Baden has a working-class history tied to the river and the industries that once defined the region. The homes here are largely mid-20th century — modest, well-built properties that have served families for generations but now need the kind of ongoing attention that any aging home requires. Common repair needs include worn flooring, deteriorating exterior trim, sticking doors and windows, and fixtures that are long past their useful life. We serve Baden and the broader Beaver County area from our base in Moon Township.
