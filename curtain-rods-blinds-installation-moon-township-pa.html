Small Job, but Worth Doing Right

Curtain rods and blinds seem simple, but heavy drapery needs hardware anchored into a stud, and blinds need to be measured and mounted precisely to operate smoothly. A rod that pulls out of drywall a few weeks after installation is a sign it was never anchored correctly.

We measure, mount into studs where possible, and make sure everything is level before we consider the job done.

Curtain Rod and Blinds Installation in Moon Township

The most common issue we see with DIY curtain rod installs is hardware anchored into drywall alone rather than a stud. It holds for a while, especially with lightweight curtains, but heavier drapery eventually pulls the anchors loose. We locate studs where possible and use proper anchors when a stud is not available.

Blinds installation is more about precision than strength — getting brackets level and properly spaced so the blinds raise, lower, and tilt smoothly. A slightly uneven install is one of those things that is subtle at first but becomes obvious and annoying over time.
