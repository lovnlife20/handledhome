In-Wall Cord Routing

Running cables inside the wall gives the cleanest look but requires cutting into drywall. This can typically be done in existing construction using an in-wall rated power kit and cable channels.

In some cases — near fireplaces, with thicker walls, or in certain home construction types — in-wall routing may not be possible or practical. Surface cord covers are a clean, code-compliant alternative.

TV Mounting FAQs

Do I need to supply the mount?

You can, or we can source one for your TV size and use case. If you already have a mount, just let us know the brand and type when you reach out.

What if studs aren't where I need them?

We can use a mounting board to bridge studs, or select a bracket placement that works with the stud layout while still getting the TV where you want it.

Can you mount a TV above a fireplace?

Yes. Above-fireplace mounting requires a tilting bracket and secure fastening into the wall structure. We handle this regularly.

Can you do other jobs in the same visit?

Yes. TV mounting is often combined with picture hanging, shelf installation, or other small jobs in one visit.
