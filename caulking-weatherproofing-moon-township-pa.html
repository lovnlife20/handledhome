Fall Is the Best Time to Do This

Pittsburgh winters are cold and long. A full weatherproofing pass before temperatures drop is one of the highest-return things you can do — lower heating bills, fewer drafts, and less moisture getting into walls.

Caulk also needs temperatures above about 40°F to cure properly, so scheduling before the cold arrives is important for getting a good seal.

Caulking & Weatherproofing FAQs

How often should exterior caulk be replaced?

Most exterior caulk lasts 5 to 10 years depending on the product and exposure. If you see cracking, gaps, or sections pulling away from the surface, it is time to redo it.

Can you do a whole-house weatherproofing pass?

Yes. We can go around the exterior and common problem areas inside in one visit and handle all the gaps, caulk, and weatherstripping at once.

Do you replace weatherstripping too?

Yes. Door weatherstripping that is worn flat, torn, or compressed no longer seals properly and is easy to replace as part of a weatherproofing visit.

When is the best time to schedule this?

Early fall is ideal — before temperatures drop below 40°F, which is the threshold for most caulks to cure properly. Late summer through October is the sweet spot.
