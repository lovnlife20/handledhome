Door & Trim Repair FAQs

Can you fix a door that sticks or will not close?

Yes. Many sticking doors can be adjusted by correcting hinge, latch, alignment, or rubbing issues.

Do you replace trim and molding?

Yes. We can repair or replace damaged baseboards, casing, molding, and other interior trim.

Can you match existing trim?

We do our best to match existing trim as closely as possible. Exact matches depend on the age, profile, and availability of the material.

Can I text photos for an estimate?

Yes. Photos are helpful. Send close-ups of the damage and a wider shot of the full door, wall, or trim area.
