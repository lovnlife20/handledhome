Door & Trim Repair FAQs

Can you fix a door that sticks or will not close?

Yes. Many sticking doors can be adjusted by correcting hinge, latch, alignment, or rubbing issues.

Do you replace trim and molding?

Yes. We can repair or replace damaged baseboards, casing, molding, and other interior trim.

We do our best to match existing trim as closely as possible. Exact matches depend on the age, profile, and availability of the material.

Door and Trim Repair in Moon Township Homes

Doors and trim are the first thing you notice when a room feels unfinished — and often the last thing that gets addressed. A door that sticks, swings open on its own, or won't latch catches your attention every single time you walk through it. Trim that's cracked, pulling away from the wall, or was painted over without proper caulking makes a room look worn even when the rest of the space is in good shape.

In Moon Township, the most common door issues in older neighborhoods are settling-related. Homes in Carnot, along Flaugherty Run, and off Thorn Run have had decades of seasonal movement, and doors that were hung level in 1975 may no longer sit in quite the same plane today. That movement shows up as sticking at the top or side of the door, latches that miss the strike plate, or doors that drift open or closed because the jamb is no longer plumb. These are very fixable problems — they usually don't require a full door replacement.

When we come out to look at a door, we diagnose what's actually causing the problem before touching anything. A sticking door might need the hinge screws tightened, the hinge mortise deepened, the top of the door planed, or the strike plate adjusted — the right fix depends on what's actually causing the bind. For trim repairs, we assess whether the piece needs to be re-secured, refilled, or replaced, and we match the profile as closely as possible to what's already there.
