About McKees Rocks

McKees Rocks is a historic Ohio River borough just west of Pittsburgh, known for its tight grid of rowhouses, working-class roots, and long community history. Many of the homes here date back to the early and mid-20th century — a mix of brick rowhouses, small single-family homes, and older doubles. These properties have real character, but they also carry decades of deferred maintenance: deteriorating trim, cracked plaster, worn flooring, aging fixtures, and doors that haven't latched right in years.

Many McKees Rocks homeowners are long-term residents who have lived in the same house for decades — and a lot of those homes show it in the best and worst ways. Solid original construction and woodwork that modern homes can't replicate, alongside plaster that has settled and cracked, windows that have never quite fit right, and kitchens with fixtures from thirty years ago that are finally being replaced. We work in McKees Rocks regularly from our base in Moon Township and are comfortable working through whatever the list looks like.

What We See Most in McKees Rocks Homes

Plaster Repair

Plaster repair is more common in McKees Rocks than anywhere else in our service area. These homes predate drywall, and the plaster on the walls and ceilings is what there is. We repair and patch plaster to match the existing surface — not treating it like standard drywall, which is a different material with different behavior. Cracks from settling, holes from fixture removal, and sections that have separated from the lath all come up regularly.

Interior Door Problems

Interior doors in older McKees Rocks homes have usually been painted shut multiple times, hung off-plumb as the house settled, or had hardware replaced so many times that the strike plates no longer align properly. Fixing them correctly often involves addressing the frame, not just the door itself — and sometimes the floor has risen enough under settled hardwood that the door never had a chance to clear it.

Full-Home Flooring Updates

Flooring in McKees Rocks homes is frequently replaced throughout the house in one project — the original hardwood or old vinyl tile finally being swapped out after decades. Old subfloors in these homes can be uneven, have residual adhesive, or have soft spots that need addressing before new flooring goes down. We assess the subfloor before any material is installed.

Exterior Trim on Brick Facades

The brick itself on McKees Rocks rowhouses is typically in good shape — brick lasts. What fails is the wood trim around it: window casings, brick mold, sills, and porch trim that have softened from decades of moisture exposure. We repair and replace exterior wood trim on older brick homes regularly, and we caulk and seal everything properly so the repair holds through Pittsburgh winters.
