About Crescent Township

Crescent Township is a small, residential community along the Ohio River in Allegheny County, sandwiched between Moon Township to the south and Sewickley to the north. It has a genuinely semi-rural character that's unusual this close to Pittsburgh — wooded hillsides, quiet streets, and the kind of privacy that draws people who want to be near the city without being in it. The housing stock is predominantly mid-20th century: ranch homes, cape cods, split-levels, and the occasional older property that predates the post-war boom.

The wooded character of Crescent Township isn't just aesthetically distinct — it affects how homes age. Tree canopy keeps moisture on exterior surfaces longer. Root systems can disturb drainage and create wet spots near foundations. Falling debris accelerates wear on decks, gutters, and rooflines. And the seasonal dampness that comes with heavy tree coverage means exterior wood — trim, deck boards, railings, siding details — tends to soften faster here than in open suburban developments. Homes in Crescent need consistent exterior attention to stay in good shape.

What We See Most in Crescent Township Homes

Exterior Wood Rot and Trim Repair

Exterior wood rot repair is the most common job we do in Crescent Township — more than anywhere else in our service area except perhaps the older river boroughs. The combination of tree coverage, consistent moisture, and older homes that haven't had exterior maintenance in years creates exactly the conditions where trim softens, fascia boards fail, and window casings start to show decay. We repair and replace exterior wood trim, caulk and prime properly, and address the underlying moisture exposure that caused the problem in the first place.

Deck Repair and Maintenance

Older decks on Crescent Township properties are frequently in need of board replacement, railing tightening, or more significant structural attention. A wooded lot with dappled shade and consistent moisture is the environment where deck lumber breaks down fastest — boards that look okay from a standing position often have significant softness when tested underfoot. We assess the full deck structure, not just the surface boards, and repair or flag anything that compromises safety.

Long-Deferred Interior Maintenance

Crescent Township's quiet, residential character has kept a lot of long-term homeowners in place for decades. These residents take pride in their homes but have accumulated a list of interior items — fixtures that have needed replacing for years, a door that's never latched right, flooring that was supposed to be temporary fifteen years ago. A comprehensive maintenance visit that works through the interior list is often what these homeowners need most.

Seasonal Door and Window Issues

The humidity variation between Crescent Township's moist summers and dry winters is pronounced enough to cause significant seasonal door and window movement in older homes. Doors that swing freely in February stick badly in August, and vice versa. Addressing this properly means planing and adjusting for the midpoint of the range — not cutting to fit in one season and creating a gap in the other. We've done enough of these in this area to know the approach that works.
