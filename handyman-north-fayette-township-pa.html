About North Fayette Township

North Fayette Township sits in southwestern Allegheny County, bordered by Moon Township to the north, Oakdale to the west, and Collier Township to the east. The township's character splits cleanly along its two main corridors. Oakdale Road runs through the older, more established parts of the township — the communities that grew up along it have homes from the 1950s and 1960s, with mature trees, settled foundations, and the kind of deferred maintenance that accumulates over decades in Pittsburgh's climate. Wood rot on trim, decks that have never been properly refinished, doors that swell every summer — those homes have a specific and familiar repair profile.

The I-79 exits brought a completely different wave of development starting in the 1980s and running through the 2000s. Subdivisions like the neighborhoods around North Fayette Community Park filled in with newer construction — two-story colonials, attached townhomes, and single-family homes on generous lots. Those homes are now 20 to 35 years old. The builder-grade carpet is finally being replaced. The original light fixtures are due for an upgrade. The deck built in 2001 needs boards replaced. It's the same renovation wave we see in Robinson and Moon Township — just with bigger yards.

That lot size matters. North Fayette homeowners call us for outdoor projects more often than almost anywhere else in our service area — shed assembly, playset installation, deck repair, and exterior maintenance are all high-volume in this township. If you've got the property, you've got the project list to match.

What We See Most in North Fayette Township Homes

Newer Home Updates

In the I-79 corridor subdivisions, the most common calls are homeowners upgrading what came with the house — replacing builder-grade carpet with LVP or laminate flooring, swapping out basic light fixtures for something that fits the space, TV mounting and smart home installation, and the general cosmetic punch list that builds up after a few years of ownership. These homes are in good shape structurally; the work is about making them feel like the owner's house rather than the builder's product.

Route 50 Corridor Maintenance

Older homes along Route 50 and in McDonald have a completely different repair profile. Decks that have been there for 30 years, wood rot on exterior trim that hasn't been properly caulked in a decade, doors that swell shut every summer, and fixtures that have never been replaced. These homes often benefit from a comprehensive visit that works through the list rather than addressing a single item at a time.

Outdoor and Shed Projects

North Fayette's larger lot sizes mean more outdoor projects than we see in the denser communities closer to Pittsburgh. Shed assembly is a high-volume job in this township — homeowners with property want storage. Playset installation for families with larger yards, deck repair and maintenance, and fence-related work are all common here.

Move-In Punch Lists

North Fayette sees a steady turnover of homes as families move in and out of the I-79 corridor communities. New homeowners often have a list of items they want addressed after closing — touch-up painting, a door that doesn't close right, a fixture that needs replacing, flooring in one room. We can work through a move-in punch list efficiently in a single visit.
