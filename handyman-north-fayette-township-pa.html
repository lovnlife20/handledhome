About North Fayette Township

North Fayette Township occupies a stretch of southwestern Allegheny County between Moon Township, Oakdale, and the Collier Township area. The township has a split character — older properties along Route 50 and the McDonald area have been there for generations, while the I-79 corridor has brought newer residential development over the past few decades. That mix shows up in the repair calls we get: older homes need the kind of attention that comes with age (deck boards, settling doors, wood rot), while newer builds are more often calling for installations, updates, and punch-list items after move-in.
