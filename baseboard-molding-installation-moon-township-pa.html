Common Baseboard & Molding Problems We Fix

  • Missing baseboard after new flooring is installed
  • Gaps between baseboard and the floor or wall
  • Outdated or mismatched trim profiles
  • Water-damaged or swollen baseboard
  • Uneven corners and open miter joints
  • Nail holes and seams that were never filled
  • No crown molding or chair rail where a room calls for it
  • Trim that was never caulked or painted

Trim Ties a Room Together

Baseboard and molding are easy to overlook until they are missing, damaged, or outdated — and then they are one of the first things people notice. A room with new flooring but old, mismatched baseboard still looks unfinished.

We measure the room, cut the trim to fit, and install it with clean corners and tight seams so it is ready for caulk and paint. If you already have a painter lined up, we can leave the trim caulked and paint-ready, or handle the touch-up paint ourselves.

How the Process Works

1. Send Photos or Measurements

Photos of the room and rough measurements help us scope the baseboard or molding project before we arrive.

2. Review Trim Style & Fit

We confirm the trim profile, height, and material, and check how it will meet existing doors, corners, and flooring.

3. Install & Finish

We cut, install, and caulk the trim, and clean up the work area when the job is done.

Baseboard & Molding FAQs

Can you install baseboard after new flooring?

Yes. Matching new baseboard to a fresh floor is one of the most common reasons homeowners call us for this service.

Do you install crown molding?

Yes, we install crown molding, chair rail, and other decorative trim in addition to standard baseboard.

Can you match my existing trim profile?

We do our best to match existing trim profiles as closely as possible, depending on the style and what is available.

Do you paint the trim after installing it?

We can leave trim caulked and paint-ready, or handle touch-up painting ourselves — just let us know what you need.

Baseboard and Molding Work in Moon Township Homes

A lot of our baseboard and molding calls come right after a flooring project. New LVP or laminate flooring goes down, and the old baseboard either does not match the new floor height or gets damaged during removal. Rather than live with a gap or mismatched trim, homeowners in Moon Township and the surrounding area have us install new baseboard that fits the finished floor cleanly.

The other common request is upgrading plain, builder-grade trim to something with more character — crown molding in a dining room, chair rail in an entryway, or wider baseboard throughout a first floor. Homes near Carnot and along Flaugherty Run often still have the original thin baseboard from when they were built, and swapping it for a taller profile is one of the more noticeable, affordable upgrades a homeowner can make.
