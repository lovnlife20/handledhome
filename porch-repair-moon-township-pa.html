The First Thing People Notice

A porch is often the first part of the house people see and the first thing you step onto, so a soft board, a loose railing, or an uneven step stands out quickly. It is also one of the areas most exposed to weather, which means it needs more frequent attention than a lot of the rest of the house.

We check whether a problem is cosmetic or structural before recommending a fix, and repair only what needs it rather than replacing an entire porch when a section repair will do.

Porch Repair FAQs

Is this the same as your deck repair service?

No, this page focuses on porches. Our Deck Repair & Building service covers deck-specific work, though the two often overlap.

Can you fix just a section of the porch instead of rebuilding it?

Yes, in most cases we repair the specific damaged section rather than replacing the whole porch.

Do you repair screened-in porches?

Yes, including screen replacement and framing repairs.

How do I know if my porch problem is structural?

Send us photos and a description of what you are seeing — sagging, bounce, or visible cracks usually point to a structural issue, and we can advise from there.

Porch Repair in Moon Township Homes

Front porches take a beating in the Pittsburgh area — rain, snow, and freeze-thaw cycles all work against exposed wood. In a lot of older homes around Moon Township, the original porch framing and decking is decades old, and a floorboard that feels soft underfoot is usually the first sign that moisture has been getting in for a while.

Railings and steps are the other area we get called out for most, often because they are noticed as a safety issue rather than a cosmetic one. A wobbly railing or an uneven step is worth fixing quickly, especially with kids, guests, or older family members using the porch regularly.
