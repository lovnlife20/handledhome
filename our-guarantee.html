What the Guarantee Covers

The guarantee covers our labor — the quality of the work itself, for one year from the day the job is completed.

Installation and Repair Work

Anything we installed, repaired, replaced, or built. If a drywall patch cracks along our seam, a deck board we fastened works loose, a door we hung starts binding, or a fixture we mounted comes down — that's on us.

Every Job, Every Size

The same one-year coverage applies whether it was a full deck rebuild or a single hour of punch-list work. Small jobs are not treated as disposable.

No Charge to Come Back

No trip fee, no diagnostic fee, no labor charge to correct covered work. We schedule it like any other job and we fix it.

Written Into the Contract

You don't have to take our word for it after the fact. The warranty terms are in the written contract you sign electronically before any work begins, so you have them in hand from day one.

What It Doesn't Cover

We would rather be straight with you now than have an uncomfortable conversation later. A workmanship warranty covers our labor — it isn't a warranty on the products themselves or on the rest of the house.

Manufacturer defects and product failures

We can guarantee that a faucet was installed correctly. We can't guarantee the faucet. Materials and fixtures are covered by their own manufacturer warranties — and if one fails, we'll help you identify the warranty and get the claim started.

Homeowner-supplied materials

You're welcome to supply your own fixtures, flooring, doors, or hardware, and our installation of them is still covered. The products themselves are not — we don't warranty defects, product failures, or missing and damaged parts on materials we didn't purchase.

Normal wear, weather, and settling

Finishes age, caulk eventually shrinks, wood moves with the seasons, and houses settle. Storm damage, impact damage, and ordinary wear over time aren't workmanship failures.

Pre-existing conditions we flagged

Sometimes the right fix is a repair on a structure that's already near the end of its life, and we tell you that before we start. When we've recommended replacement and you've asked us to repair instead, the repair itself is covered — but the underlying condition we warned you about isn't.

Later changes by others

If our work is modified, moved, or worked on by someone else afterward, or damaged by misuse, we can't stand behind what's there now. We're happy to come look either way.

How to Use It

There's no claim form, no portal, and no phone tree.

1. Call or Text Us

Reach us at 412-353-5341 or info@handledhome.net. A photo of the problem is the fastest way to show us what's going on.

2. We Look at It

We'll come out and see it in person. If it's our workmanship, we schedule the fix. If it turns out to be something else, we'll tell you honestly what it is and what it would take to address.
