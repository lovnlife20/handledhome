About Moon Township

Moon Township is a growing community in western Allegheny County, situated along Route 60 just minutes from Pittsburgh International Airport and about 15 miles west of downtown Pittsburgh. The township has developed steadily over the past several decades, adding residential neighborhoods and commercial corridors while maintaining established older areas that have been here since the mid-twentieth century. That mix of housing ages — from homes built in the 1960s and 70s in areas like Carnot and along Flaugherty Run, to newer developments closer to University Boulevard — translates directly into a steady and varied demand for home repairs, updates, and general maintenance.

Neighborhoods and Areas We Serve in Moon Township

We serve homeowners throughout Moon Township, including established neighborhoods and newer developments alike.

Thorn Run & Flaugherty Run

The corridors along Thorn Run Road and Flaugherty Run Road run through some of Moon's more established residential areas. Homes here tend to be older and have the kind of repair needs that come with age — decks that need boards or railings addressed, drywall that has been patched improperly before, doors that have settled, and exterior trim showing wear.

Carnot

Carnot is one of the older settled areas of Moon Township, with a mix of single-family homes on larger lots. Homes here commonly need door adjustments, drywall work, deck repairs, and the kind of maintenance that comes with decades of seasonal movement and normal wear.

Route 60 & University Blvd Corridor

The residential developments along and off Route 60 and University Boulevard include some of the newer construction in the township. Homeowners here often need installations, TV mounting, smart home setup, and punch-list items after move-in. New homes still get drywall damage and small repairs — they just tend to need a different mix of work than older homes do.

Common Handyman Needs in Moon Township Homes

The type of work that comes up most often depends a lot on when the home was built. Older and newer housing stock in Moon Township tend to need different things.

Older Established Neighborhoods

Homes from the 1960s through the 1980s in areas like Carnot, along Flaugherty Run, and off Thorn Run tend to need the kind of work that comes with age. Drywall cracks that have been patched too many times, exterior doors that have settled out of square, deck boards that have rotted or split, and exterior trim with wood rot around windows and soffits are all common calls. These homes have good bones, but they need regular attention to stay in shape.

Newer Developments

Homes in newer Moon Township developments — particularly off Route 60 and University Boulevard — tend to need a different mix. TV mounting, smart home installation, ceiling fan swaps, shelving, furniture assembly, and punch-list items from move-in top the list. New construction isn't immune to drywall damage and small repairs either, but the emphasis is more on getting the home set up and livable the way the owner actually wants it.
