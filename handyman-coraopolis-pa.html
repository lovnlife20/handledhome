About Coraopolis

Coraopolis is a small Allegheny County borough tucked between Moon Township and the Ohio River, with Fifth Avenue running through its commercial center and residential streets climbing the hillside above. The housing stock reflects two distinct eras: early 20th century worker housing near the borough center, built when the area supported river and rail industry, and postwar bungalows spread across the hillside streets above. Many of these homes are solidly built but have decades of deferred maintenance, and the topography creates its own set of repair needs.

Homes built from the 1920s through the 1950s make up a large portion of Coraopolis housing. Original woodwork and trim are still common in many of these properties, and the hillside location means basement moisture and foundation settling are recurring concerns. Decks and porches added over the decades are often past their useful life, with rotted boards, soft posts, and railings that no longer meet code. Interior repairs like drywall patches, door adjustments, and fixture replacements are regular requests in these older builds.

My home is on one of the hillside streets and the foundation seems to be settling. Can you help?

We see this regularly in Coraopolis. Hillside homes are prone to seasonal movement that shows up as doors that stick in summer and swing open in winter, cracks at window corners, and floors that feel slightly uneven. We handle the resulting repairs: door adjustments, trim gaps, drywall cracks, and related finish work. We do not do structural foundation work, but we can address the visible symptoms and help you understand what is cosmetic versus what needs a structural contractor.
