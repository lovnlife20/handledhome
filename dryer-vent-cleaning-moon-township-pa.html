Common Signs Your Dryer Vent Needs Cleaning

  • Clothes taking longer than usual to dry
  • The laundry room feels hotter than normal during drying cycles
  • A burning smell when the dryer runs
  • Visible lint buildup around the dryer or vent opening
  • The vent has never been cleaned since the home was built
  • Excess lint in the lint trap even after cleaning it
  • The exterior vent cap flapper does not open properly
  • A long or bent vent run that is harder to clean yourself

A Maintenance Item Worth Not Skipping

Dryer vent fires are one of the more common and preventable home fire risks, and lint buildup is almost always the cause. Beyond the safety angle, a clogged vent also makes your dryer work harder and longer, which adds wear and increases energy costs.

We clean the full length of the vent, not just the section right behind the dryer, since lint tends to accumulate at bends and the exterior cap just as much as near the source.

Dryer Vent Cleaning FAQs

How often should a dryer vent be cleaned?

Most homes benefit from cleaning once a year, though heavy laundry use or long vent runs may need it more often.

How do I know if my vent needs cleaning?

Longer dry times, a hot laundry room, or a burning smell during drying cycles are the most common signs.

Can you clean long or bent vent runs?

Yes, we clean the full length of the vent regardless of bends or distance to the exterior wall.

Is this really a fire safety issue?

Yes. Lint buildup is one of the leading causes of home dryer fires, which is why regular cleaning is worth staying on top of.
