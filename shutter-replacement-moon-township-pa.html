Shutter Replacement FAQs

Do I need to supply the new shutters?

Yes — you purchase the shutters you want and we install them. That way you get the exact color, style, and size you want without any guesswork.

Can you match shutters to my existing ones?

If you send us a photo, we can help you identify the style and size. You can then order matching replacements before we come out.

Will removing old shutters damage my siding?

We remove shutters carefully to minimize damage. Any holes left behind can be filled and touched up as part of the job.

Can you combine shutter replacement with other repairs?

Absolutely. Many homeowners combine shutter replacement with exterior trim work, caulking, or other exterior projects in the same visit.

Shutter Replacement in Moon Township and the Pittsburgh Area

Shutters are one of the first things a visitor notices about a house, and faded, cracked, or missing ones undercut the rest of a home's exterior no matter how well-kept everything else is. Vinyl shutters — which is what most Moon Township homes have — are durable but not permanent. Sun exposure on south- and west-facing walls fades them over several years, and Pittsburgh's freeze-thaw cycles work mounting hardware loose over time, leaving shutters that are visibly crooked or pulling away from the siding.

The typical shutter replacement project in Moon Township involves replacing all the front shutters with new vinyl in a matching or updated color. Sometimes the call comes because a single shutter cracked or blew off in a storm and can no longer be found in the same color — which often means replacing all the front shutters to match. Other times homeowners just want a refresh: an updated color, a different louver style, or getting rid of shutters the previous owners installed in a size or style that never really fit the house.

When we do shutter replacement, we remove the old shutters, assess the mounting points on the siding, and install the new shutters level and evenly spaced on both sides of each window. Mounting hardware is replaced or upgraded as needed — old corroded shutter locks and holdbacks don't come back. We space the shutters consistently across the full front of the house so the result looks intentional rather than piecemeal.
