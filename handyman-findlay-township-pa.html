About Findlay Township

Findlay Township is one of the largest townships in southwestern Allegheny County, and it has a notably different character from the denser river communities to the east. Pittsburgh International Airport occupies a significant portion of the township, and the surrounding area has seen substantial residential and commercial development since the airport opened in its current location in 1992. The newer subdivisions near the airport — Hankey Farms and similar communities along the Route 60 and Route 30 corridors — brought a wave of homeowners who work in airport-related industries or who wanted affordable single-family homes close to major highway access.

Older Findlay Township is a different story. Route 30 west of the airport passes through older small communities with mid-20th century housing that has its own repair and maintenance character — aging decks, settling doors, worn flooring, and exterior trim that's been neglected. Findlay Township residents often drive further to get to anything, so finding a reliable local handyman is something they genuinely value.

What We See Most in Findlay Township Homes

Airport Corridor New Construction Updates

Homeowners in the newer Findlay Township subdivisions near Pittsburgh International Airport are often at the 15–25 year mark of ownership — the point where builder-grade finishes start looking tired and homeowners are ready to update. Flooring replacement (carpet to LVP or laminate), fixture upgrades, TV mounting, smart home installation, and cosmetic updates throughout the house are common calls from this part of the township.

Large Lot Outdoor Projects

Findlay Township homes typically sit on larger lots than the river boroughs to the east. More land means more outdoor projects: shed assembly for storage, playset installation for growing families, deck repair and maintenance on structures that have been exposed to western Pennsylvania weather for a decade or two, and caulking and weatherproofing on homes with more exterior surface area to maintain.

Route 30 Older Home Maintenance

The older Route 30 corridor homes in Findlay Township have traditional deferred maintenance needs — decks that need board replacement, exterior trim that has softened and needs repair, doors that have settled out of alignment, and fixtures that are finally being replaced after years of use. One visit covering several of these items is the most efficient way to catch up on a home that's been running behind on maintenance.

First-Time Handyman Calls

Findlay Township is somewhat isolated from the denser Pittsburgh suburbs — there's less foot traffic of contractors and service people than you'd find in closer-in communities. We regularly hear from Findlay Township homeowners who have been meaning to find a reliable handyman for years and haven't found one that follows through. We respond to inquiries, provide estimates, and show up when scheduled — which matters more than it should have to.
